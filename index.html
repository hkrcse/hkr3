About Me

Hi, Rupok here. I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh University of Business and Technology (BUBT), Dhaka. My research interests lie in the intersection of Blockchain Systems, Zero-Knowledge Proofs, Cryptography, Cyber Security, and the Internet of Things. My current work focuses on privacy-preserving digital identity, using zero-knowledge proofs so that identity claims can be verified without disclosing the underlying personal data.

Before joining BUBT, I was a Lecturer at Shanto-Mariam University of Creative Technology, Dhaka. I graduated from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), where I received the Dean's Award three times and ranked 10th in my class.
