About Me

Hi, Rupok here. I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at The University of Alabama, Tuscaloosa, working with Dr. Lina Pu. My research interests lie in the intersection of Embedded Security, Cybersecurity, Blockchain Systems, Zero-Knowledge Proofs, Cryptography, and Secure System Design.

Before starting my Ph.D., I was a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh University of Business and Technology (BUBT), Dhaka, and before that a Lecturer at Shanto-Mariam University of Creative Technology. I graduated from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), where I worked on blockchain-based identity systems with Prof. K. M. Azharul Hasan and received the Dean's Award.

Alongside research, I enjoy teaching and mentoring students, organising cybersecurity competitions, and building practical CTF-style learning environments.

Balanced-Augmented KDD IDS: Robust Classification with Explainable AI and Leakage-Safe Pipelines

An intrusion detection approach on the KDD data that corrects class imbalance through augmentation, keeps the training pipeline free of data leakage, and uses explainable AI to make its classifications inspectable.

Paper Link

EcoSupplyChain: A Blockchain-Enabled Dual-Flow Framework for Counterfeit Prevention, Profit Transparency, and Sustainable Recycling

A dual-flow supply chain framework on blockchain that tracks goods forward for counterfeit prevention and backward for recycling, while keeping profit distribution across participants auditable.

Md. Humayan Kabir, Rakibul Hasan, Md. Shafiqul Islam

Published in ICCIT 2025, 28th International Conference on Computer and Information Technology

Paper Link

BCVVS: Blockchain-Based CV Verification System for Secure and Tamper-Proof Credential Validation

A credential verification system that anchors CV claims on a blockchain so that an employer can confirm a candidate's qualifications against a tamper-evident record rather than a self-reported document.

Md. Humayan Kabir Rupok, K. M. Azharul Hasan

Published in ICCA 2024, Proceedings of the 3rd International Conference on Computing Advancements

Paper Link

BDIMS: A Blockchain Based Digital Identity Management System with Zero Knowledge Proof

A digital identity system in which a holder proves an identity claim to a verifier without revealing the underlying credential, combining an on-chain registry with zero-knowledge proof verification.
